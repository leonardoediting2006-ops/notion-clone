# Sound effects: real recordings, cleaned and cut

Every sound in this folder is a cut of a real field recording (freesound.org, via the ESC-50 dataset and Sonic Pi's CC0 sample
library). Nothing is synthesised. They are the same cleaned cuts the dog-gaze short uses (made by `tools/forge-doorway-sfx.py`,
`tools/forge-clothes-sfx.py` and `tools/forge-gaze-sfx.py`); the cue sheet that places them on the picture is the end of `main.js`.
