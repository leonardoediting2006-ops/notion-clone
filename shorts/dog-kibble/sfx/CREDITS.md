# Sound effects: real recordings, cleaned and cut

Every sound in this folder is a cut of a real field recording (freesound.org, via the ESC-50 dataset and Sonic Pi's CC0 sample
library). Nothing is synthesised. Most are the cleaned cuts of the dog-gaze library (`tools/forge-doorway-sfx.py`,
`tools/forge-clothes-sfx.py`, `tools/forge-gaze-sfx.py`); the crunches, chews and gulps are new (`tools/forge-kibble-sfx.py`).
The cue sheet that places them on the picture is the end of `main.js`.
