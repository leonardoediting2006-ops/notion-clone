# Sound effects: real recordings, cleaned and cut

Every sound in this folder is a cut of a real field recording (freesound.org, via the ESC-50 dataset and Sonic Pi's CC0 sample
library). Nothing is synthesised. The sounds were made by `tools/forge-doorway-sfx.py`, `tools/forge-clothes-sfx.py` and
`tools/forge-gaze-sfx.py` (spectral-gating noise reduction, band-limiting, fades, level); the cue sheet that places them on the
picture is the end of `main.js`.
